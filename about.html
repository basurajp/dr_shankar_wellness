About Dr. Shankar Wellness

Traditional wellness practices, explained with care.

Namaste. I’m Shankar Hugar, the practitioner behind Dr. Shankar Wellness in Vijayapura, Karnataka. I have been practicing for four years and also offer online consultations for people who prefer to connect from home.

Our approach

Dr. Shankar Wellness focuses on drugless complementary practices, general wellness education and practical conversations about everyday routines. We begin by understanding what you want to discuss, explain the proposed session, and let you decide whether it feels right for you.

Practices and guidance

Depending on your needs and preferences, services may include acupressure, colour therapy, TCM-informed practices, auricular therapy, crystal therapy, marma therapy, Sujok and seed practices, food and nutrition guidance, and advanced numerology. Numerology is offered separately as a personal-reflection service.

Kannada wellness education

On YouTube and Facebook, Dr. Shankar shares Kannada videos and updates about wellness, traditional practices, lifestyle and nutrition. Online content is general information; it is not a personal diagnosis or treatment plan.
