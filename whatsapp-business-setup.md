## Before submitting any verification request

- Use a WhatsApp Business account controlled by the business and secure it with two-step verification.
- Make the WhatsApp display name, profile image, website, Facebook Page and Instagram details consistent with the real business identity.
- Complete the missing facts below, then ensure the same facts appear on the public site and the WhatsApp profile.
- Keep business registration or other official business evidence, and documents proving control of the phone number, available in case Meta requests them. Submit only documents and business information Meta specifically requests in the in-app flow; accepted evidence can differ by verification product and account.
- If pursuing Meta Verified, check the WhatsApp Business app's Settings/Business Tools for availability and follow the current in-app application. A badge is subject to Meta's review and may be a paid subscription.
- Include the public policies page on the site and a working contact route. Ensure privacy, service descriptions, fees, and medical-scope wording are accurate.
