Privacy Policy

How we handle information you choose to share.

This Privacy Policy applies to Dr. Shankar Wellness, operated by Shankar Hugar in Vijayapura, Karnataka. It describes information received through this website, email, phone, WhatsApp, social media, and consultations.

Information you may share

When you contact us or request a consultation, you may choose to share your name, phone number, email address, town, appointment preferences, questions, symptoms, or other details relevant to your enquiry. For an online consultation, you may also choose to send a tongue photograph if requested. We may keep basic appointment and payment confirmation records. Do not send card details, passwords, identity documents, or information unrelated to your enquiry.

How we use it

  • Reply to questions and arrange appointments.
  • Understand the subject you want to discuss and provide the service you requested.
  • Confirm fees, payments, rescheduling, and follow-up.
  • Maintain appropriate business records and comply with applicable requirements.

Who may process information

We do not sell your personal information. If you contact us through WhatsApp, email, Facebook, Instagram, Telegram, or another service, that platform processes information under its own privacy terms. Payment information is handled by the payment service you use; we do not ask you to send payment credentials in chat. Information may also be disclosed when required by law or needed to protect safety and legal rights.

Health information and group messages

Symptoms and photographs can be sensitive. Share them only in a private conversation with Dr. Shankar Wellness, and only if you are comfortable doing so. Do not post personal health details in public comments or WhatsApp groups: other members may see or retain group messages.

Storage and retention

We keep information only for as long as reasonably needed to respond, provide consultations, manage follow-up, and maintain business records. Messaging services and device backups may retain messages according to their own settings and policies. You can ask us to delete copies we control, subject to any records we must retain.

Website links and technical data

This website links to external services including Google Business Profile and social platforms. When you follow an external link, that provider’s terms and privacy policy apply. The website host may process standard technical information such as page requests and device/browser details to deliver and secure the site.

Your choices

You can choose what information to provide, stop messaging us, leave a WhatsApp group, or ask us to correct or delete information we control. Contact us using the details below. We may need to verify a request and may retain information where required by law or for legitimate business records.
